* Inspect cabinet and back panel on handover from machine shop
 * Dimensions
 * Hole locations
 * Back plate fit
 * Finish quality (evenness, burrs, nicks)

* Attachment of CLD layer
  * Prepare back panel for gluing by scuffing the interior face with 400 grit sandpaper then wiping with IPA
  * Cut 520x220mm section of 0.8mm nitrile rubber and clean one face with IPA
  * Spray both faces to be glued with kwik-grip
  * Wait 2 minutes to allow to get tacky
  * place rubber on work surface glued face up
  * place backplate on rubber glued face down
  * apply light pressure for 1 minute
