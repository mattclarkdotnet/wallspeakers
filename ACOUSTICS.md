* 2 x Dayton LW150-4 woofers (86dB/2.83V, Re=3.6, parallel Re=1.8)
* 1 x Tectonic TEBM28C10 (80dB/2.83V, Re=3.9)
* 500x200x48mm aluminium cabinet
* 400Hz crossover

### Driver sensitivity matching

The two woofers have a combined sensitivity (being closely spaced and seeing the same signal) of 86+6=92dB/2.83V, at the cost of a reduced Re of 1.8.

Therefore at the crossover frequency the woofer level needs to be reduced by 12dBSPL, or 24dBV

However a Linkwitz Transform is applied to the woofer, which has a 10dBV gain.  We will likely also want to apply up to 10dB of loudness compensation and/or room correction for low level listening to the bass region.  Therefore the 24dBV drop at the crossover region will work out well to provide the needed bass headroom.

### Driver power handling

The woofers have a Linkwitz transform applied to move f3 to 54Hz and f6 to 42Hz.
